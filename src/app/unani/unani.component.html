About Unani Medicine

Unani medicine is a traditional system of healing that originated in ancient Greece and was further developed by Arab and Persian scholars. Based on the theory of the four humors (blood, phlegm, yellow bile, and black bile), it emphasizes maintaining balance in the body through diet, herbal remedies, detoxification, and lifestyle changes. This holistic approach addresses physical, emotional, and environmental factors to promote well-being and longevity.
