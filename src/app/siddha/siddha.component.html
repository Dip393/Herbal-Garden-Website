About Siddha Medicine

Siddha medicine, one of the oldest systems of traditional medicine, originated in Tamil Nadu, India. Developed by the Siddhars (ancient spiritual scientists), it is based on the principles of the five elements (Earth, Water, Fire, Air, Ether) and the three humors (Vatham, Pitham, and Kapham). Siddha emphasizes the use of herbs, minerals, and lifestyle practices for curing diseases and promoting longevity. This holistic system integrates physical, mental, and spiritual well-being.
