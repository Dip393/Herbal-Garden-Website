About Homeopathy

Homeopathy is a natural system of medicine founded in the late 18th century by German physician Dr. Samuel Hahnemann. It is based on the principle of "similia similibus curentur" or "like cures like," which means a substance that causes symptoms in a healthy person can be used to treat similar symptoms in an ill person when administered in a highly diluted form.
