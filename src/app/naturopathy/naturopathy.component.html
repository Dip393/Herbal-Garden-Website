About Yoga & Naturopathy

Yoga and Naturopathy are interconnected approaches that focus on self-healing through natural means. Yoga, a practice originating in ancient India, combines physical postures, breathing techniques, and meditation for mental and physical wellness. Naturopathy emphasizes natural therapies, including diet, hydrotherapy, and fasting, to detoxify and rejuvenate the body. Together, they promote a balanced, drug-free lifestyle for overall health and harmony.
